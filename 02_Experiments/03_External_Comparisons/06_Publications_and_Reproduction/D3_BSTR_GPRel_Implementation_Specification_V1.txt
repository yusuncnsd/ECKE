BSTR-GPRel D3: paper-to-code implementation specification V1

1. Publication
Entity and relation joint extraction from materia medica classics based on BSTR-GPRel. Shen Wang, Sun Ke, Ma Ruolin, Li Xin, Bian Wei. Library and Information Service,2026,70(6):30–39. DOI:10.13266/j.issn.0252-3116.2026.06.003. The journal PDF was examined; no official code was provided. Paper-based faithful reimplementation (R3).

2. Inputs and outputs
Original inputs are cleaned Tangye Bencao, Shennong Bencao Jing and Diannan Bencao, represented by WordPiece, position/segment and Jieba POS features. Outputs are subject span, object span and relation triples supporting multiple relations and overlap.

3. Architecture
BERT WordPiece provides 768-dimensional contextual features with position/segment embeddings; concatenate trainable Jieba POS embeddings. BSTR uses BERT plus multilayer Transformer bidirectional self-attention, dynamic training masks and controlled embedding perturbation for adversarial training.
Three GlobalPointer levels: entity subject/object boundary detection with RoPE, relation-specific head matrices connecting entity starts, and relation-specific tail matrices connecting entity ends. Jointly optimize entity/head/tail tasks.

4. Loss
Each task uses Focal Loss: L_entity,L_head,L_tail. Total loss is alpha*L_entity+beta*L_head+gamma*L_tail. The paper describes dynamic balancing but does not supply initial weights, update rules or Focal Loss alpha/gamma values. These must be identified as implementation choices fixed within development, not invented as published settings.

5. Decoding
For a relation to form a triple, entity spans and relation-specific head/start and tail/end pairings must agree. The structure supports an entity in multiple triples. Thresholds, conflict order and tie handling were unreported and must be frozen using development only.

6. Published and omitted settings
BERT hidden768; max sequence256; BERT lr3e-5; AdamW; max30 epochs; early stop after five validation-F1 nonimprovements; original train/validation/test8:1:1; PyTorch2.1.0, Python3.10, AutoDL. Batch, weight decay, warmup, seed, adversarial method/strength, mask rate, POS dimension, added Transformer depth, pointer head size/threshold and loss weights were not reported.

7. Study adaptation
Use seven entity types and seven relation patterns, 100 development training records and 300 frozen test inputs. Retain WordPiece/POS fusion, BERT/Transformer, adversarial training, three-level GlobalPointer and multitask loss. Do not add ECKE lexicon anchoring, topic-boundary controls or LLM decisions. Predefine small development candidate sets for omitted settings; freeze choices and evaluate the final model on test data.

8. Comparability
This is the closest D-method background: ancient TCM materia medica, joint extraction and overlapping relations. With no author code and underspecified weights/thresholds/adversarial details, call it faithful reimplementation, not official implementation.
